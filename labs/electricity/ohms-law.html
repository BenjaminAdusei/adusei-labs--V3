📖 The Crucial Difference: Ohm's Law vs. Circuits

It is very common to confuse "Ohm's Law" with "Circuits", but they are entirely different concepts in physics.

Ohm's Law (V = I × R)

This is a fundamental physical rule for a specific material. It dictates how electricity behaves inside a single, isolated component (like the resistor above). It simply states that current is directly proportional to voltage.

Circuits (Series & Parallel)

A circuit is the physical structural layout of the entire electrical system. It dictates how multiple components connect together (topology). You use Ohm's Law as a tool to solve a circuit, but the circuit itself is the pathway, not the law.
