📖 Understanding Magnetism

Magnetic Field (B): A region where magnetic forces act. Field lines go from North to South (outside the magnet).

Bar Magnet: Has two poles — North (N) and South (S). Like poles repel, opposite poles attract.

Electromagnet: A coil of wire carrying current. Magnetic field is produced — strength depends on current and number of turns.

Compass: A small magnet that aligns with Earth's magnetic field. It points North (toward Earth's magnetic South pole).
