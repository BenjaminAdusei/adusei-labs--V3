📖 Understanding Electromagnetic Induction

Faraday's Law: ε = -N × dΦ/dt
The induced EMF is proportional to the rate of change of magnetic flux.

Lenz's Law: The induced current flows in a direction that opposes the change that produced it. The negative sign in Faraday's Law.

Generator: Converts mechanical energy to electrical energy. Rotating coil in a magnetic field produces alternating current (AC).

Transformer: Vₛ/Vₚ = Nₛ/Nₚ (voltage ratio equals turns ratio). Step-up: Nₛ > Nₚ, Step-down: Nₛ < Nₚ.
