📖 Hooke's Law & Elasticity

Hooke's Law: F = kx
The force needed to extend a spring is directly proportional to the extension. k is the spring constant (N/m).

Elastic Potential Energy: E = ½kx²
Energy stored in a stretched or compressed spring. Depends on both k and extension squared.

Elastic Limit: The maximum force before permanent deformation. Beyond this, the material no longer obeys Hooke's Law.

Real-world Applications:
• Vehicle suspension — springs absorb shocks
• Weighing scales — spring extension measures weight
• Bungee jumping — elastic cord stores and releases energy
