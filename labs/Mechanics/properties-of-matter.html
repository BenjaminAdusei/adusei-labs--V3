📖 Understanding Properties of Matter

Density: ρ = m/V (kg/m³). Mass per unit volume. Density determines whether objects float or sink.

Upthrust (Archimedes' Principle): Fᵦ = ρfluid × Vdisplaced × g. The buoyant force equals the weight of displaced fluid.

Viscosity: Internal friction in fluids. Viscous force depends on velocity gradient and fluid properties.

Surface Tension: Force per unit length acting at the surface of a liquid. Causes droplets to form spheres.

Pressure in Fluids: P = ρgh. Pressure increases with depth in a fluid.
