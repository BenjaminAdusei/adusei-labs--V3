📖 Understanding the Gas Laws

The gas laws describe how the physical properties of a gas—pressure (P), volume (V), and temperature (T)—are related to each other.

Boyle's Law (T constant): P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
Pressure and volume are inversely proportional. When volume doubles, pressure halves.

Charles's Law (P constant): V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
Volume and temperature are directly proportional. When temperature doubles, volume doubles.

Pressure Law (V constant): P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂
Pressure and temperature are directly proportional. When temperature doubles, pressure doubles.

Ideal Gas Equation: PV = nRT
Combines all three laws into one equation. R = 0.0821 L·atm/(mol·K).
