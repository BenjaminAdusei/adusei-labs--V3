📖 Understanding Sound Waves

Sound is a longitudinal wave that travels through a medium (air, water, solids). Key properties include frequency, wavelength, amplitude, and speed.

Wave Equation: v = fλ
Speed of sound in air ≈ 343 m/s at 20°C. Increases with temperature.

Pitch & Loudness:
Pitch is determined by frequency (higher f → higher pitch). Loudness is determined by amplitude (larger A → louder sound).

Doppler Effect:
Apparent frequency changes when source and observer are in relative motion. Moving toward → higher pitch; moving away → lower pitch.
