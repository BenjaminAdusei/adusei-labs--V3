📖 Understanding Wave Phenomena

Interference: Two or more waves superpose to form a resultant wave. Constructive (in phase) or destructive (out of phase).

Young's Double Slit: Δy = λL/d, mλ = d sin θ

Diffraction: Bending of waves around obstacles or through slits. Single slit: a sin θ = mλ (minima).

Polarization: Restriction of wave oscillations to one plane. Malus's Law: I = I₀ cos²θ.

Standing Waves: Result of two waves traveling in opposite directions. Nodes (zero displacement) and antinodes (maximum displacement).
