📖 Understanding Atomic Physics

Bohr Model: Electrons orbit the nucleus in discrete energy levels (n=1,2,3...). Energy is quantized.

Energy Levels: Eₙ = -13.6 / n² eV. Higher n means higher energy (less negative). Ionization occurs at E=0 eV.

Atomic Spectra: When electrons transition between levels, photons are emitted or absorbed. λ = hc / ΔE.

Spectral Series: Lyman (UV, n≥2→1), Balmer (Visible, n≥3→2), Paschen (IR, n≥4→3).
