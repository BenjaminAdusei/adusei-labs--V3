📖 Understanding Radioactivity

Radioactive Decay: Unstable nuclei emit radiation to become stable. Types: Alpha (α), Beta (β), Gamma (γ).

Half-Life (t₁/₂): The time for half of the radioactive nuclei to decay. N = N₀(½)^(t/t₁/₂).

Decay Types:
• Alpha (α): Helium nucleus (²⁴He) — low penetration, high ionization.
• Beta (β): Electron or positron — medium penetration.
• Gamma (γ): High-energy photon — high penetration, low ionization.

Activity (A): The rate of decay. A = λN, measured in Becquerels (Bq). λ = ln(2) / t₁/₂.
